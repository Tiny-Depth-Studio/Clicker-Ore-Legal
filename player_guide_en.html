Play With Every
Parameter Explained

This guide walks through the real numbers and mechanics behind pickaxes, skills, pets, prestige, bosses, and the store. It's built to answer questions like "When does prestige unlock?", "Which pickaxe should I upgrade?", and "Is it worth fighting this boss?"

Quick Strategy Summary
The three stages below follow the game's natural progression flow — where to focus at each point.
Early Game (Floor 0–200)
  • Keep upgrading your pickaxe; buy the next one the moment it unlocks
  • Unlock OreBreaker at floor 50 and AngerClick at floor 70
  • Buy your first pet (Rocky) as soon as you can afford it
Mid Game (Floor 200–350)
  • Prestige for the first time at floor 200; put Essence into DPS and Click Damage first
  • Unlock the Skill Tree (floor 180) and start stockpiling SkillStone
  • Get ready for the Boss system as you approach floor 350
Late Game (Floor 350+)
  • Stock up Dungeon Keys and fight bosses regularly (your main SkillStone/Essence source)
  • Save diamonds for the Infinity items (Power/Income/Armor/Clicker)
  • Grow all 7 prestige parameters evenly each run instead of maxing just one
01

Currencies

There are 7 currencies in the game. Each feeds a different system — hoarding one and spending it in the wrong place is the most common early mistake.

Gold

Earned: breaking ore

Spent: pickaxe & suit upgrades, most purchases

Diamond

Earned: boss rewards, ads, real-money purchase

Spent: buying pets, Infinity items, store

Essence

Earned: only from Prestige rewards

Spent: upgrading the 7 Prestige Parameters

Taskium

Earned: +1 per click, task completion

Spent: Taskium store

Dungeon Key

Earned: daily reward (1/day), store packs

Spent: entering a boss fight (1 per attempt)

SkillStone

Earned: boss rewards

Spent: Skill Tree nodes

OreStone

Earned: 1–5 random per ore broken

Spent: this is also the achievement reward currency; used in the OreStone system

Good to know
Once numbers get big enough, the game switches to scientific notation like 1.23e45 (mantissa × 10exponent). The largest representable value is roughly 1e308 — a value that hits this ceiling stops growing and stays fixed there.
02

Click Damage & DPS (Auto Damage)

You have two separate damage sources: every manual click (Click Damage) and automatic per-second damage (DPS). They're fed by different things.

What increases click damage?

Roughly in order of impact: your active pickaxe's level and skills, the AngerClick skill (×2 to ×5 while active), the Prestige parameter "Stonecrusher's Hand," title bonuses, and converting a slice of your DPS into click damage (unlocked/strengthened by the Prestige parameter "Unity of Force").

What increases DPS (automatic per-second damage)?

Your pickaxe's DPS stat (especially DPS-focused pickaxes), the Rampage skill (×2 to ×3.5 while active), the Prestige parameter "Eternal Forge," and your pets' combined DPS. One important detail: pet DPS is added after every multiplier is applied — it's added raw, not scaled by them. That means pet levels alone aren't enough in the late game; you still need to grow your pickaxe/skill multipliers.

See the exact formula (for advanced players) 
Click Damage = 1
  + (Pickaxe Click Damage + share converted from DPS)
    × Infinity × Temperature × AngerClick × Title × Prestige multipliers

Total DPS = (Pickaxe DPS × Infinity × Temperature × Rampage × Prestige × Title multipliers)
            + Total Pet DPS  (pet DPS is not affected by these multipliers, it's added last)
03

Critical Hits

Every click has a chance to land as a critical hit, dealing more damage than normal.

StatBase (no upgrades)
Critical Chance5%
Critical Damage Multiplier×1.5

There are four ways to boost these: the CriticalStrike skill (multiplies chance by ×1.3–×1.9 while active), critical bonuses from pickaxe skills, the Prestige parameters Ore Oracle (chance) and Cataclysmic Force (multiplier), and title bonuses. When a crit lands, that click's damage is multiplied by the total critical multiplier.

04

Pickaxe Guide

There are 45 pickaxes in the game (not 17 — we checked while writing this guide). Each unlocks in sequence; some contribute to Click Damage, others to DPS.

When does a new pickaxe unlock?

A pickaxe automatically unlocks once your total gold earned this prestige run crosses a threshold. That threshold scales with the pickaxe's purchase cost — so reaching the next pickaxe usually means upgrading your current one well enough to earn the gold, not just clicking faster.

How does the purchase cost scale?

The first pickaxe is a flat 4 Gold. Every pickaxe after that costs roughly 6 times more than the previous one — which is why buying several pickaxes in a row is usually less efficient than fully upgrading the one you already have.

Upgrade cost per level

Each level costs about 7% more than the last. That means endlessly upgrading a single pickaxe gets harder and harder — the "unlock a new pickaxe" flow exists specifically to balance that curve.

Key Power Spike
Every time a pickaxe reaches a multiple of 100 levels, its value gets multiplied by ×11. Pushing one pickaxe toward level 100, 200, 300... ahead of the others usually gives the single biggest power jump available.

Per-pickaxe skill tree

Each pickaxe has its own 6 skills. Skills become purchasable once that pickaxe reaches a specific level (e.g. 5, 20, 50, 100...) and grant a permanent percentage bonus to click damage, DPS, critical chance/multiplier, or gold income. Some skills only affect that one pickaxe, while others boost the combined total of all pickaxes — the global-effect skills are generally the more valuable ones.

What drives pet DPS?

Each pet level adds roughly 1% of your current total click damage × the pet's order number as DPS. That's why Shadowshard (order 5) is five times stronger than Rocky (order 1) at the same level — but leveling Shadowshard also costs more. Practical takeaway: grow your click damage first (that's what pet DPS is based on), then invest in pet levels.

Level cost scales exponentially with a pet's order and grows roughly 30% per level — so in the late game, prioritizing your highest-order (strongest) pet is usually more efficient than keeping every pet at the same level.

Each pet also has its own 6 skills, unlocking at set levels; some only affect that pet, others boost the combined total of all pets.

Prestige Warning
Prestiging resets every pet's level back to 1 and un-purchases their skills. You don't need to buy the pets themselves again, though — ownership is permanent.
07

Ore & Depth

As you progress, floors get tougher but pay out more gold. Depth (in meters) is what determines which ore types you'll encounter.

What changes as floors get harder?

Each floor has slightly more health and slightly more gold than the last — growth starts slow and accelerates the further you go. Also, every 10th floor ("duration floor") turns into a mini-boss: a 20-second timer starts, and that ore's health/gold is boosted by roughly 50% (health) / 51% (gold).

What determines ore type?

The type of ore you break (Iron, Copper, Gold, Uranium, etc.) depends entirely on depth (meters), not the floor number. New, rarer ore types get added to the pool as depth increases:

DepthTotal Ore TypesExamples
0–100m9Iron, Copper, Carbon, Silicon...
100–500m20+ Gold, Silver, Lead, Zinc...
500–1500m30+ Platinum, Titanium, Cobalt...
1500m+40+ Uranium, Thorium, Osmium (rare)

Ore type only affects appearance and its "dust" reward — how tough it is to break and how much gold it pays out is determined entirely by the floor, not the ore type.

Every broken ore also drops 1 to 5 random OreStone.

See the exact formula (for advanced players) 
Ore Health = 1 + 9 × 1.06^(floor-1) × floor        (×1.5 every 10th floor)
Gold Reward = 1 + 1.45 × 1.05^(floor-1) × floor    (×1.51 every 10th floor)
Depth(m)    = 1 + 1.1 × 1.01^(floor-1) × floor
08

Prestige

Prestige resets most of your progress in exchange for a permanent power source (Essence). Timed well, it speeds up your progress overall.

When does Prestige unlock?

The Prestige panel unlocks the first time you reach floor 200. The requirement grows for each subsequent prestige: (your current prestige level + 1) × 100. So after your 1st prestige, the 2nd requires floor 300, the 3rd requires floor 400, and so on.

How is the Essence reward calculated?

It's based directly on what you accomplished during that prestige run:

  • Highest floor reached × 0.5
  • Total ore broken × 0.25
  • Pickaxe levels bought × 0.1
  • Suit levels bought × 0.1
Worked Example
You reached floor 250, broke 5,000 ore, and bought 300 pickaxe levels and 50 suit levels:
(250×0.5) + (5000×0.25) + (300×0.1) + (50×0.1) = 125 + 1250 + 30 + 5 = 1,410 Essence
As you can see, breaking ore is by far the biggest Essence source — spending your last minutes before prestiging racing to a higher floor is usually less valuable than just breaking as much ore as possible.

Where should you spend Essence? (The 7 Prestige Parameters)

These bonuses stack additively level by level (not compounding), meaning every level contributes an equal amount — so your investment "doesn't get diluted" even at low levels. Cost is in Essence and rises slowly as level increases (roughly a 1% growth rate plus the level itself), meaning these 7 parameters are far cheaper to grow than pickaxe or pet upgrades — it usually pays to spend Essence regularly rather than let it pile up.

Boss Fights

Bosses are a timed damage race: kill the boss before the timer runs out for a big reward, or walk away with nothing if you don't.

How do you unlock and enter one?

The Boss/Dungeon system unlocks at floor 350. It doesn't start automatically — you pick a boss from the dungeon entry panel and spend 1 Dungeon Key to start the fight. No key means no fight, so keeping track of your key stock matters.

10 Bosses, unlocked in order

Bosses aren't random — beating one unlocks the next in a fixed sequence. Health increases roughly 1000× per level, while the time limit shrinks.

If You Lose
If the boss is still alive when the timer hits zero, the fight ends with no reward and the Dungeon Key you spent isn't refunded. Before entering, make sure your DPS can realistically finish that boss's health bar within the time limit — you'll see the remaining health percentage after a failed attempt, which helps you estimate how much more DPS you need for next time.

Boss rewards are the main source of SkillStone and Essence — growing your Skill Tree and Prestige parameters requires fighting bosses regularly.

10

Dungeon Key

It has exactly one job: your ticket into a boss fight.

Every boss attempt costs 1 key (win or lose, it's not refunded). Your key sources: 1 free key from the daily reward, plus purchasable packs of 3 / 7 / 15 in the store. Since boss rewards (SkillStone, Essence) are a core late-game resource, it pays to avoid burning keys on attempts you're not actually ready to win.

There are 25 achievement categories (breaking ore, click count, critical hits, boss kills, prestige count, and more), most with 3–6 tiers. Every achievement reward is paid in OreStone.

General pattern: both the target and the reward roughly scale ×10 per tier. A few examples:

12

Daily Reward

Simple, and there's no penalty for missing a day.

Once a day, logging in gets you 1 Dungeon Key. The claim resets at UTC midnight. There's no 7-day reward calendar and no login streak — missing a day carries no penalty, and your claim simply resets normally the next day. You need to be logged in (online) to claim it.

Save & Security — "Will I Lose My Progress?"

Short answer: no, several layers protect your progress.

  • The game auto-saves, encrypted, every 15 seconds.
  • It backs up to Steam Cloud roughly every 45 seconds (and always when the game closes or is paused) — with Steam Cloud enabled, you can pick up right where you left off on a different computer.
  • If a save file gets corrupted, the game automatically falls back to the last backup file with no progress loss.
  • You can also manually copy your save to the clipboard (export) and paste it back in on another device (import).
