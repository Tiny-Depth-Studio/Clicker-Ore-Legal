Player Guide - 2026-09-14

Everything In The Mine,
In Plain Words

What each system does, when it opens up, and where your next hour is best spent. No formulas - just the decisions that matter.

01

Where To Start

Three stages, following the order the game opens things up in.

Early - floor 1 to 100
  • Upgrade your pickaxe constantly; new pickaxes open as you reach deeper floors.
  • Ore Breaker arrives at floor 50, Anger Click at floor 100.
  • Click the gold balloon whenever it floats in, and buy Rocky once you can spare the diamonds.
Middle - floor 100 to 350
  • Prestige once you have passed floor 100 and progress slows down.
  • Tasks open at floor 125, armour at 130, the skill tree at 180 and the Trader at 200.
  • Spread Essence across the 7 parameters instead of maxing one.
Late - floor 350 and beyond
  • Fight bosses whenever you hold a key; they pay Essence and Ore Stone.
  • Enchantment materials start dropping at floor 500, workers join from floor 600.
  • Keep armour levelled so heat never eats your damage.
Good to know
Numbers get long fast, so the game switches to short forms and then to scientific notation like 1.23e45. Nothing is capped - that is only a change of notation.
02

The 7 Currencies

Each one feeds a different system. Spending the right currency in the wrong place is the most common early mistake.

Gold

Earned by breaking ore, plus balloons, workers, tasks and offline time.

Spent on pickaxes, upgrades and item skills - your main workhorse.

Diamond

From real-money purchases, the Lucky Spin and promo codes.

Spent on pets, Infinity items, time skips, chests and extra spins.

Essence

From prestige, boss wins and the Trader.

Spent on the 7 permanent prestige parameters.

Taskium

+1 for every successful click.

Spent on accepting and rerolling tasks.

Dungeon Key

One free key a day, plus store packs and the Lucky Spin.

Spent to enter a boss fight - 1 per attempt.

Skill Stone

From store packages and the Trader.

Spent on levelling the 8 active skills.

Ore Stone

1-5 from every ore you break, plus achievements and bosses.

Spent on trades, enchantment crafting and worker upgrades.

Server currencies
Diamonds and dungeon keys are held on the server, so buying with them needs you to be online. If the connection dropped, the game reconnects by itself when you try to buy.
03

Click Damage And Auto Damage

Two separate damage sources, fed by different things.

Click damage is what a tap does. It grows with your first pickaxe's level and skills, the Anger Click skill, your title, the Stonecrusher’s Hand prestige parameter and enchantments. A slice of your auto damage is also folded into every click once Unity of Force is levelled.

Auto damage (per second) comes from the second pickaxe onward, the Rampage skill, the Eternal Forge parameter, enchantments and your pets. It is dealt continuously while an ore is alive.

Pets are the exception
Pet damage is added after most multipliers - heat, titles, prestige and boosts do not scale it; only Premium does. Pet levels alone will not carry you late game.
04

Critical Hits

Every click can land as a critical and hit harder.

You start with a 5% chance and a x1.5 multiplier. The Critical Strike skill, pickaxe skills, the Ore Oracle and Cataclysmic Force prestige parameters, titles and enchantments raise them. Critical chance stops at 100%, and the prestige parameter cannot be bought past that point.

05

Pickaxes

45 pickaxes, unlocked by depth. They are the backbone of your damage.

  • A pickaxe unlocks by floor: the second at floor 15, the tenth at floor 782, the last at floor 13,624. The gaps grow the deeper you go.
  • The first pickaxe costs 5 gold, the second 50, and each one after that about 8 times more.
  • Every level costs about 6% more than the previous one.
  • Only the first pickaxe feeds click damage. All the others feed auto damage per second.
  • Each pickaxe has 6 skills. They show up and unlock along a ladder of levels - 5 / 25 / 125 / 625 / 3,125 / 15,625 - so the last ones are long-term goals. Some help that pickaxe, some help all of them.
The big jump
From level 100, and again every 150 levels (250, 400, ...), a pickaxe raises its own output to a power: ^1.05, then ^1.1, and so on. On big numbers that is a huge gain, so pushing one pickaxe to its next threshold is usually the best upgrade you have.
Prestige locks them again
Prestige locks and resets every pickaxe; they reopen as you pass their floors in the new run.
06

Armour

5 suits, all owned from the start. They exist to keep the heat off you.

  • You never buy armour - you only level it. Each level adds about 5% heat resistance, starting from 50, and costs about 8.4% more than the last.
  • Suits use the same power jump as pickaxes, from level 100 and every 150 levels after.
  • Each suit has 6 skills on the same level ladder (5 / 25 / 125 / 625 / 3,125 / 15,625).
  • The armour panel opens at floor 130, together with the heat gauge.
07

Heat

The deeper you go the hotter it gets, and heat quietly cuts your damage.

  • Floor temperature climbs steadily - roughly 1.04x per floor, so it doubles every couple of dozen floors.
  • While your resistance is at least 1.5x the floor temperature, the gauge stays clear and nothing is lost.
  • Once resistance drops below the temperature, damage starts bleeding away; at 0.5x you keep only a twentieth of it - a 95% loss.
  • The armour panel has an auto-buy switch for resistance; leave it on if you push depth often. Heat only exists in the main mine, not in boss fights.
If progress stalls
A sudden wall where ore takes forever is almost always heat, not damage. Check the gauge before you spend anything else.
08

Pets

5 pets, bought with diamonds and then levelled with gold.

  • Prices run from 150 to 250 diamonds (150 / 175 / 200 / 225 / 250).
  • Each level adds about 1% of your pickaxe click damage as auto damage, multiplied by the pet's order - so Shadowshard gives five times what Rocky gives at the same level.
  • Levels get about 20% more expensive each time, and pets share the pickaxe power jump from level 100.
  • Each pet has 6 skills; some help only that pet, some help all of them.
  • The pet panel opens at floor 30, or as soon as you own a pet.
Prestige and pets
Prestige puts every pet back to level 1 and clears their skills. Ownership is permanent though - you never rebuy a pet.
09

Workers

5 helpers that mine for you while the game is open.

  • Workers join as you pass floors 600 / 800 / 1,000 / 1,200 / 1,400. That counts your best floor ever, so they never lock again.
  • Give each worker a pickaxe - a pickaxe can only work for one of them at a time, and your own damage is not reduced. A worker without a pickaxe earns nothing.
  • Income is added every 1 second and waits in a pool until you press collect. A stronger pickaxe earns more.
  • Levelling a worker costs Ore Stone - 100 for the first level, about 2% more each time - and adds about 1% income per level.
Collect before you prestige
Workers only earn while the game runs, not offline. Prestige keeps their levels but clears uncollected income and takes back their pickaxes.
10

Enchantments

Craft enchantments from materials you dig up, then fit them to pickaxes, suits and pets.

Breaking ore can drop enchantment dust and enchantment pieces in 4 tiers. Each tier starts dropping once your highest floor this run passes its threshold. Farming more than 100 floors below your best floor lowers the chance, down to 10% of normal.

Tier Stats Drops from floor Dust chance Piece chance Craft (dust / piece / Ore Stone)
Common 1 500 5% 0.5% 10 / 1 / 40
Rare 1-2 1,000 2% 0.1% 20 / 1 / 200
Epic 2-3 2,000 1% 0.03% 30 / 1 / 1,000
Legendary 3-4 4,000 0.5% 0.01% 50 / 1 / 5,000
  • The Crafter turns dust, one piece and an Ore Stone fee into an enchantment with random stats. Higher tiers roll more stats and multiply them harder (x1 / x2 / x4 / x6).
  • Every pickaxe, suit and pet has 8 slots, opening at floors 1,000 / 3,000 / 6,000 / 9,000 / 12,000 / 15,000 / 18,000 / 21,000. Those use your best floor ever, so a slot never closes again.
  • Some stats help only the item they sit on (click damage, auto damage, heat resistance); others help everything (gold, critical hits, DPS share, upgrade cost). A special stat boosts the item's own skills instead, so it only works on items with bought skills.
  • The enchantment picker only lists enchantments that would actually do something on that item.
  • In the Trader, 10 dust or pieces of one tier become 1 of the next tier. You can also dismantle an enchantment you do not want for dust (5 / 10 / 15 / 25 by tier) - dismantling is permanent.
Prestige is safe
Prestige never deletes enchantments. If an item gets locked again, its enchantments simply return to your Inventory, which opens at floor 500.
11

Floors, Ore And Depth

Floors decide how tough ore is and how much gold it pays. Depth decides what the ore looks like.

  • Every floor has a little more health and a little more gold than the one before, and the curve steepens as you go.
  • Every 10th floor is a timed floor: a 20 second clock starts, health is x5 and gold x2.
  • Difficulty also runs in cycles of 100 floors - some floors in a cycle pay better relative to their health than others.
  • Every ore drops 1-5 Ore Stone on top of its gold.
  • 5 rare ores (Ferrovyx, Karnyth, Thalveris, Umbryssa, Zephiris) can take the place of a normal ore. The odds run from about 1 in 10,000 down to 1 in 1,000,000, and once you break one it is recorded for good and never spawns again.
Timed floors bite
If the clock runs out you are pushed back one floor and auto-advance turns off. Farm a little before trying again.

The Elevator takes you back to any floor you have reached this run. Picking a floor by hand turns auto-advance off; turn it back on when you want to climb again. The Locations panel switches between the main mine and the boss dungeon.

There are 40 ore types in total, and depth decides which ones appear. Ore type only changes the look - health and gold come from the floor.

Active Skills

8 skills, each a timed boost followed by a cooldown.

A skill is free when its floor arrives - you start using it at level 1. Levelling it up to level 7 costs 63 Skill Stone in total, or 504 for all 8. Higher levels mean longer duration and shorter cooldown.

Skill Floor Level 1 (duration / cooldown) Level 7 (duration / cooldown) Effect
Ore Breaker 50 30s / 540s 90s / 180s 7 → 13 /s
Anger Click 100 40s / 660s 210s / 300s x2 → x5
Critical Strike 150 30s / 600s 150s / 240s x1.3 → x1.9
Rampage 200 40s / 840s 160s / 480s x2 → x3.5
Golden Frenzy 250 50s / 1,020s 290s / 660s x2 → x5
Heat Resistance 300 30s / 660s 140s / 300s x2 → x5
Overcharge 350 20s / 1,200s 20s / 840s x1.5 → x3
Time Reversal 400 1,200s 840s -300s → -1,020s

The first skill line opens at floor 50; the Skill Tree where you spend Skill Stone opens at floor 180. Skill levels survive prestige.

The combo
Fire Overcharge first, then the skill you actually want boosted - for example Golden Frenzy. Time Reversal then cuts the cooldown of whatever you used last, so you get it back sooner.
13

Titles

100 titles that pick themselves up as you climb.

  • Titles run from floor 1 to floor 50,000 and are chosen automatically from the highest floor you reached this run.
  • Each title adds about +0.1 to the bonuses it carries.
  • The bonuses arrive in stages - click damage, auto damage, heat resistance, gold, critical multiplier, critical chance and the DPS-to-click share start at titles 2 / 6 / 11 / 16 / 21 / 26 / 31.
  • Prestige takes you back to the early titles; they return as you climb again.
14

Tasks And Taskium

A side track that turns your clicking into a second income.

  • Every successful click gives +1 Taskium.
  • Accepting a task costs 200, rerolling one costs 25.
  • The task panel opens at floor 125; the 6 slots open at floors 125 / 225 / 325 / 425 / 525 / 625.
  • There are 10 task types in 6 rarities. Each task lasts 60 minutes, and unaccepted ones are replaced every hour.
  • The gold reward scales with rarity - between 50-500 times the ore gold of your best floor this run. The rarest ones also pay Essence.
15

Daily Missions

A short list of goals that refreshes every day.

  • You get 5 missions a day - break ore, click, spin the wheel, beat a boss, use skills and so on.
  • Each finished mission pays a 10 minute x2 boost (auto damage, click damage, critical hits or gold).
  • Rewards are not automatic - claim them from the mission panel. The list resets at midnight UTC.
16

Trader

Opens at floor 200. The place where Ore Stone and spare materials turn into something useful.

  • 1 Skill Stone becomes 5,000 Ore Stone.
  • 2,000 Ore Stone becomes 1 Essence.
  • 5,000 Ore Stone becomes 1 Skill Stone.
  • Each currency trade charges a flat 100,000 gold fee. Material trades and dismantling charge Ore Stone instead.

The Ore Stone to Skill Stone trade matters most: it is how a long farming session turns into skill levels without a store purchase.

17

Free Rewards

Sources that ask for little more than showing up.

  • Gold balloon - from floor 50, one drifts in every 500-700 seconds and waits until you click it. It pays x6 the ore gold of your best floor this run.
  • Gold Crystal - from floor 100, one shows up every 800-1,300 seconds. Clicking it in the main mine swaps the ore for a 10 second bonus ore with x10 health and x14 gold, paid out hit by hit.
  • Offline earnings - counted after 60 minutes away and capped at 12 hours. Premium raises it.
  • Play time reward - every 60 minutes of play pays about 100 times a minute's worth of gold at your best floor.
  • Daily reward - 1 free dungeon key a day from the store. No streak to protect; you need to be online.
  • Lucky Spin - from floor 100, an 8-slice wheel that refreshes every 3 hours with 1 free spin. Extra spins cost diamonds, starting at 10 and doubling each time. Won slices stay closed until the wheel refreshes; prizes include gold that scales with your floor, Ore Stone, boosts, Taskium, 50 diamonds and dungeon keys.
  • Promo Codes - codes we share in announcements and the community go into Settings > Promo Codes. Each code works once per account, and some have a limited number of uses.
18

Prestige

Trade this run's progress for permanent power. Timed well, it is the fastest thing in the game.

The prestige panel shows up at floor 70. You can prestige once you pass floor 100 in the current run - every run asks for the same floor.

  • Reset: gold, floor progress, pickaxe, suit and pet upgrades and their skills, skill cooldowns, title progress, Taskium, task progress, worker pickaxe assignments and uncollected worker income.
  • Kept: diamonds and everything bought with them, dungeon keys, dungeon progress, Essence, prestige parameters, skill tree levels, Skill Stone, Ore Stone, enchantments and inventory, achievements, statistics, leaderboards and worker levels.
  • Every run starts again from floor 1, with no starting gold.

The Essence reward depends only on the highest floor you reached, and it grows fast: about 331 at floor 200, 1,098 at floor 300, 12,068 at floor 500 and 4,827,341 at floor 1,000.

Go a little deeper
Because the reward climbs so steeply with depth, a few more floors before you prestige are usually worth more than prestiging again right away.
Parameter Effect Per level
Eternal Forge Auto damage per second +2.5%
Stonecrusher’s Hand Click damage +2.5%
Golden Abyss Gold income +1%
Ore Oracle Critical chance +0.1%
Cataclysmic Force Critical multiplier +0.1%
Infernal Shield Heat resistance +1%
Unity of Force Share of DPS added to clicks +0.1%

Each level compounds with the ones before it. A level costs 2 Essence to start and about 2.5% more each time, so spending Essence often beats hoarding it.

Bosses

A timed damage race for Essence and Ore Stone. Opens at floor 350.

  • Open the Locations panel, pick the dungeon, choose a boss and spend 1 dungeon key to start. Nothing starts by itself.
  • Bosses unlock in order - beat one to open the next. Health climbs steeply while the clock gets shorter.
  • Boss progress is kept through prestige, and you can refight any boss you have already beaten.

20

Achievements

100 achievements across 31 categories - clicks, ore, bosses, prestige, depth, crafting and more.

Every achievement pays Ore Stone, and both the target and the reward grow tier by tier.

Claim them
Rewards are not automatic. If you have not opened the achievements tab in a while, there is a pile waiting there - the claim-all button takes everything at once.
21

Store

Diamonds, keys, packages, premium time, time skips, chests and the permanent Infinity items.

  • Diamond packs: 200 / 550 / 1,150 / 2,400 / 6,250 / 14,000 diamonds. The bigger the pack, the better the rate.
  • Key packs: 3 / 7 / 15 dungeon keys.
  • Small Package: 500 Diamond, 100 Essence, 10 Skill Stone, 3 Dungeon Key.
  • Big Package: 1,000 Diamond, 200 Essence, 20 Skill Stone, 5 Dungeon Key.
  • 7-Day Premium Package: 7 days of x5 click damage, x5 auto damage, x3 gold, x3 heat resistance and x2 offline earnings.
  • 30-Day Premium Package: 30 days of x7 click damage, x7 auto damage, x4.5 gold, x3.5 heat resistance and x2.5 offline earnings.
  • Time skip: 8 / 16 / 24 hours for 100 / 190 / 370 diamonds. The reward is worked out from your own damage, floor and workers - including ore, enchantment drops and rare ores. Boosts, cooldowns and timers do not move, and it is not available inside the dungeon.

Tracking Your Progress

Places to see how the run is going and what just happened.

  • Steam leaderboards track clicks, click damage, prestige count, bosses killed and highest floor.
  • The statistics screen keeps a long record - earnings, spending, clicks, crafting, time played and more.
  • The Activity Log lists what happened this session - rewards, unlocks, bosses, purchases - and the corner notification opens it.
  • The Updates panel shows the latest patch notes in your language and opens by itself once after a new update.
23

Saves

Short answer: your progress is safe in several ways at once.

  • The game saves itself, encrypted, every 15 seconds.
  • It backs up to Steam Cloud about every 45 seconds, and always when you close or pause - so another computer picks up where you left off.
  • If a save file is damaged the game falls back to its backup by itself.
  • You can also copy your save to the clipboard and paste it back in on another device.

Languages

English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, Latin American Spanish, Italian, Polish, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Ukrainian, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Thai, Indonesian and Vietnamese.
